# Challenge: S-Pankki "Personal Economic Trainer"

> What the S-Pankki challenge asks for, what it values, and how it is judged.

**Last verified:** 2026-11-14

## The ask
Use AI to turn personal banking data into a trusted financial coach: people chat with their own money. Built on a large synthetic transaction dataset modelled on real S-Bank data (see [dataset.md](dataset.md)).

Example questions from the brief, from simple to deep:
- "How much did I spend on taxis last month?"
- "What kind of spender am I?"
- "What is safe for me to spend this week?"
- "How much could I save if I reduced dining out by 20%?"

## What S-Pankki values
- **Pedagogy: the three baskets.** All spending falls into:
  1. mandatory and regular (e.g. rent),
  2. mandatory and irregular (e.g. a broken washing machine),
  3. everyday spending that slips through your fingers (e.g. coffees).
  Solutions that help people understand where their money really goes "will go far".
- **MyData spirit:** let users explore their own data freely, not fixed charts the bank picked.
- **Conversational AI** that explains financial concepts in plain language, simulates "what if" scenarios, and guides toward healthier habits, e.g. **safe-to-spend** and **safe-to-save** guidance.
- **Benchmarks** against open population data (e.g. Statistics Finland): encouraged, not mandatory.
- **Audience:** everyone, with a stress on financial literacy for young adults.
- **Output:** a mobile app, or a web app that can be embedded in a mobile app. Customers are on mobile (S-mobiili).
- Optional bonus: a **multi-bank view** under PSD2 (the bank fetches the customer's accounts at other banks). Not required.

## Context
- S-Bank: Finland's 4th-largest bank, 3.5 M customers. S-mobiili is used weekly by one in three Finnish adults and is a top-rated finance app.
- To S-Pankki's knowledge, no Finnish bank yet lets customers chat with their own financial data.
